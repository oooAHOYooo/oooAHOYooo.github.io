About AHOY.

AHOY is a home for independent music and media: a place to discover local work. We build software and put on shows around the city of New Haven and beyond. We are in the process of getting our sugar together -

Built close to home.

Since late 2019, AHOY has been putting on events around New Haven and making room for artists, musicians, podcasters, filmmakers, and other independent creators. The project grows from the belief that local work deserves a durable place to be found, shared, and remembered.

Today, AHOY is becoming a baby ecosystem: Discover is our web app for finding media; Player helps organize your content and mp3 collections and to listen offline and also lo key get back to the days of owning the music; and Cards explore a physical way to hold onto the work.

AHOY was created and is actively maintained by Alex Gonzalez. We are currently working toward becoming a nonprofit, with the goal of making the project more accountable to and useful for the community it comes from.
