Ahoy Indie Media

Ahoy Indie Media is a New Haven streaming platform for independent music, podcasts, events, and films. It is built to feel local, ad-free, and direct, with support flowing back to creators and communities.

What it is

A home for local listening and watching: curated music, in-house podcasts, live events, and short-form video centered on New Haven creators.

What to expect

Full platform home-coded app that will get better with time. Dedicated community of artists and developers. Local people trying to make.

How it works

Sign up. Listen to music, podcasts, the radio, watch video and live tv - all from indie creators. If you want to support you could "boost" where all of the artists get all of the proceeds.

1
Create an account

Open the app, choose Sign Up, and follow the prompts to get started.

2
Explore local media

Browse music, podcasts, live events, and video from New Haven and nearby

3
Use the wallet to support creators

The wallet is used for in-app support and purchases so fans can boost artists directly.

4
Find shows

Use Events or the New Haven page to see upcoming shows, venues, and schedules.
